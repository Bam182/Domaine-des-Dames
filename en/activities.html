Activities & Stays

The Ardèche,
a thousand ways to be amazed

From Domaine des Dames, set off to discover the treasures of the Ardèche. In the heart of Vallon-Pont-d'Arc, at the entrance to the famous Ardèche Gorges, enjoy an exceptional setting where nature, adventure and discovery meet. Canoeing, hiking, canyoning, charming villages or river swimming… Everyone has their own way of experiencing the Ardèche.

Activities & Discoveries

The must-sees around Domaine des Dames

Vallon-Pont-d'Arc is the ideal starting point for discovering the Ardèche Gorges, a protected nature reserve, and their surroundings. As a family, with friends or in a group, let yourself be tempted by our selection of experiences and discoveries to enjoy around the estate.

Canoeing

April to October

Discover the famous Ardèche Gorges by canoe and paddle beneath the iconic Pont d’Arc. Several routes of 8, 13, 24 or 32 km let you enjoy spectacular scenery in the heart of unspoilt nature. A must-do activity, with family or friends, to suit your wishes and your level.

Canyoning

April to September

Explore the canyons of the Ardèche with natural slides, water jumps and abseiling. Led by professional guides, canyoning combines adventure, thrills and the discovery of wild landscapes. Several routes are available depending on your level and your wishes.

Treetop adventure

April to October5 min from the estate

Climb high and set off on an adventure among the trees, a stone’s throw from the Ardèche Gorges. With courses for all levels, open from age 2, treetop adventure is an ideal way to share a fun, active moment with family or friends in an unspoilt natural setting.

Hiking

All year round

Explore the Ardèche on foot along the many waymarked trails close to the estate. From a simple stroll to more demanding hikes, discover varied landscapes and magnificent viewpoints over the Ardèche Gorges. A great chance to enjoy nature and discover the region at your own pace.

Cycling

All year round

Cycle through the Ardèche and discover its characterful villages, vineyards and must-see sites. Close to the estate, the Via Ardèche greenway and many quiet cycle paths make an ideal setting for rides with family or friends. Mountain bikers can also enjoy numerous routes through the region’s varied landscapes. Classic and electric bikes can be rented right next to the estate.

Caves

All year round

Discover the underground treasures of the Ardèche through its many caves. The most famous, Grotte Chauvet 2, offers a replica of the original UNESCO World Heritage cave and its 36,000-year-old prehistoric paintings. Other remarkable sites, such as the Aven d’Orgnac or the Cocalière cave, take you into a fascinating world of underground galleries and spectacular natural formations.

Historic villages

All year round

Discover the characterful villages of southern Ardèche. Stroll the picturesque lanes of Balazuc, Vogüé, Labeaume or Ruoms, among stone houses, medieval heritage and charming little squares. Each village has its own identity and invites you to discover the history, traditions and authenticity of the region.

Local markets

All year round

Stroll through the emblematic markets of southern Ardèche. Meet local producers and artisans, discover regional specialities and enjoy the warm atmosphere of traditional markets. In summer, night markets extend these moments of discovery and conviviality.

Stays & Getaways

Tailor-made stays

Whether you love wide open spaces, cultural discoveries or simply want to unwind, Domaine des Dames is the ideal starting point to discover all the riches of the Ardèche.

Because every stay is different, we will be delighted to share our favourite addresses, our personal recommendations and our advice to help you discover the region as you wish.
