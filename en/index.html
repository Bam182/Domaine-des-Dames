The Estate

An Ardèche farmhouse
outside of time

In Vallon-Pont-d'Arc, at the gateway to the Ardèche Gorges and close to the Pont d'Arc, Domaine des Dames is a 17th-century stone farmhouse nestled among the vines. Peaceful yet right next to the activities, the estate is just a 10-minute walk from the centre of Vallon-Pont-d'Arc.

Five charming holiday cottages with a pool, a restaurant with the flavours of the embers and spaces for your group events: one place to stay, enjoy characterful cooking and celebrate in the Ardèche.

Restaurant

Characterful, authentic and generous cooking, guided by fire and a love of good produce. Cooking over embers sets the tone, while the flavours of the Ardèche terroir elevate every plate.

At Domaine des Dames, the pleasure of good food is shared in a convivial atmosphere, where indulgence meets the gentle way of life of the southern Ardèche.
